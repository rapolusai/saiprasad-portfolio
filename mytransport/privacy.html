Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MyTransport (package com.rapolus.transport), an Android app operated by Rapolus IT Services, Hyderabad, Telangana, India (“we”, “us”), handles information. In short: your business records stay on your phone. We do not run servers that receive your records, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

1. Information you enter

You may enter your name, mobile number and business name; vehicles and drivers; customers’ names and phone numbers; trips, payments, expenses, fuel, repairs, loans and EMIs; and vehicle paper details, with optional photos or PDFs of papers. All of this is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not receive, see or store it.

2. PIN and fingerprint

Your 4-digit PIN is kept on your device to lock the app. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; the app never receives fingerprint or face data.

3. Subscriptions and payments

Paid plans are sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only asks Google Play which plan is active on your Google account. We do not receive your card, UPI or bank details.

4. Internet use

The app uses the internet only to talk to Google Play (to show plan prices, start a purchase and check your plan). All record keeping works offline.

5. Camera, files and notifications

When you choose “Take photo” or “Choose photo or PDF” for a paper, the file is copied into the app’s private storage on your device. Reminders for paper expiry, EMIs and follow-ups are created on your device.

6. Backups and sharing

If you create a backup, the app writes a file encrypted with a password you choose to a place you pick (for example Downloads or Google Drive). Data leaves your phone only when you choose to export a report, share a paper or save a backup. We do not sell or share your information.

7. Children

MyTransport is for business users aged 18 and above.

8. Deleting your data

Delete records in the app, use More › Settings › Privacy and my data › Delete all my data, or uninstall the app. See Delete your data.

9. Contact

Questions: support@rapolu.in.
