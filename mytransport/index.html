MyTransport

MyTransport is an Android app for lorry, truck, tipper, tractor, bus, taxi and JCB owners in India. Record trips and bills, payments, expenses, fuel and mileage, repairs, loans and EMIs, and vehicle papers with reminders, and see profit for each vehicle. Available in English, Hindi and Telugu.

What you can do

  • Add trips with customer, amount, vehicle and money collected; see who still owes you.
  • Track diesel, labour, tolls, repairs and other expenses; fuel mileage per vehicle.
  • Loans and EMIs split into interest and principal.
  • Insurance, fitness, permit, PUC and road-tax reminders, with a photo or PDF of each paper.
  • Reports by vehicle, customer and month, as PDF or CSV.

Plans

5-day free trial inside the app. Then Basic ₹99/month (up to 2 vehicles) or Business ₹299/month (any number of vehicles), billed through Google Play.
