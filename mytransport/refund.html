Cancellation and Refunds

MyTransport subscriptions are sold and billed through Google Play.

1. Cancel anytime

Open the Play Store app › your profile › Payments and subscriptions › Subscriptions › MyTransport › Cancel. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you paid for, and it will not renew.

2. Refunds

Subscription refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy. Request a refund through Google Play (play.google.com › Order history, or the Play Store app). For help, email support@rapolu.in with your Google Play order number.

3. Free trial

The 5-day trial inside the app is free; no payment is taken for it.
