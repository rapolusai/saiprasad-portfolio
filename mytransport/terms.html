1. The service

MyTransport helps you keep records for your vehicles and transport business. Profit, dues, mileage and EMI figures are calculated from the data you enter; check important numbers before using them for tax, legal or financial decisions.

2. Your data

Your records are stored on your device. Please keep backups. We are not responsible for data lost because a device is lost or reset, or the app is removed without a backup.

3. Free trial and subscriptions

New users get a 5-day free trial inside the app; no payment is taken for it. After the trial, adding new records needs a plan: Basic ₹99 per month (up to 2 vehicles) or Business ₹299 per month (any number of vehicles). Plans are billed by Google Play and renew every month until you cancel in the Play Store. Without an active plan you can still view your records, change settings and make a backup.
