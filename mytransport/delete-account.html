Account · MyTransport by Rapolus IT Services

Delete your MyTransport account and data

MyTransport (package com.rapolus.transport) has no online account. Your name, mobile number and all business records are stored only on your phone, so you can delete them yourself at any time.

1. In the app

  • Open MyTransport and unlock it.
  • Go to More › Settings › Privacy and my data.
  • Tap Delete all my data and confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, PIN, vehicles, customers, trips, payments, expenses, papers and attached files from the device.

2. Uninstall

Uninstalling MyTransport removes all of its data from the device. Backup files you saved yourself (for example in Downloads or Google Drive) are not removed automatically; delete them if you no longer want them.

3. Ask us

Email support@rapolu.in with the subject “MyTransport delete my data”. We do not hold your records on our servers, so we will guide you through the steps above within 7 days.

4. What is kept

We keep no copy of your app data. Subscription purchase records are kept by Google Play under Google’s policies; cancel the subscription in the Play Store if you no longer need it.
