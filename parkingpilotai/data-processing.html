Data Processing Agreement

These terms supplement the ParkingPilotAI Terms between Rapolus, Hyderabad, Telangana, India, and the business customer operating the registered parking place. They apply when Rapolus processes personal data on that customer's behalf. A separately signed agreement prevails where it expressly varies these terms. Publication alone does not prove that an existing customer accepted a revised contract.

1. Scope and roles

The customer determines the purposes of its parking operations and is responsible for its lawful collection and use of operational personal data. Rapolus processes that data to deliver the customer's configured service. Where applicable, these are the Data Fiduciary and Data Processor roles. Rapolus is separately responsible for processing it determines for its own billing, account security and legal obligations.

Processing covers collection, recording, storage, retrieval, calculation, receipt delivery, reporting, archiving and deletion for the service period and permitted retention period. People concerned include vehicle owners, receipt recipients, customer administrators, managers and agents. Data can include names, mobile numbers, vehicle numbers, roles, location assignments, entry and exit times, collections and transaction references. Payment credentials and unnecessary sensitive information must not be entered.

2. Customer instructions

The contract, account settings, authorised feature use and verified written support requests document the customer's instructions. The customer must provide required notices, establish a lawful basis, obtain consent where required, keep data accurate and limit staff access. Rapolus will process operational data only for these instructions or as required by law, and will raise an instruction it reasonably believes is unlawful. Rapolus does not sell this data or use receipt contacts for its own marketing.

3. Confidentiality and safeguards

Rapolus must limit access to authorised people who need it for their duties and are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations. Safeguards must be appropriate to risk and include role-based access, tenant and location separation, protected transmission, restricted administration and audit records. The customer must protect credentials, manage staff access, secure its devices and report suspected compromise. These terms do not represent a security certification or guarantee that incidents cannot occur.

4. Providers and processing locations

The customer authorises the service providers identified in the Privacy Policy for the functions described there, including hosting, WhatsApp delivery, crash reporting, optional AI assistance, email and support. Rapolus must use appropriate contractual data-protection obligations for providers processing on its behalf and remains responsible for its contractual obligations when using them. Providers such as payment platforms may also determine their own processing under their terms.

Primary application hosting is AWS Mumbai. Other providers may process relevant data outside India; transfers must comply with applicable restrictions. Rapolus will notify customers of a material new processing provider through available account or support channels before its use where practicable. Customers may raise a reasoned data-protection objection with support; the parties will consider an alternative, and any unresolved closure or refund is handled under the service terms and applicable law.

5. Requests and incidents

Rapolus must reasonably assist the customer with access, correction, deletion and other lawful requests concerning operational data. Requests must be verified before disclosure. If a request is received directly from a vehicle owner or staff member, Rapolus may coordinate with the customer without restricting rights available under law.

Rapolus must inform an affected customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach involving its operational data, subject to lawful restrictions, and provide available information about scope, likely effects, containment and recommended actions. Further updates follow as facts become available. Each party must meet any notification duties and deadlines applicable to it and cooperate on containment; initial notice need not wait for a completed investigation.

6. Retention, export and deletion

The Privacy Policy describes active records, archives, receipt delivery logs and retention exceptions. Masking active records does not itself erase archived copies. Rapolus must assess whether identifiers remain necessary rather than automatically retaining every field for a financial-record period.

On closure, Rapolus must return or delete customer personal data as reasonably instructed, subject to lawful retention and the account-deletion process. Request available exports before closure. Bespoke exports or migration work require agreement. Retained records must have restricted use and access; backups may expire through their normal cycle, and restored data must respect completed deletion requests. Rapolus will explain the reason for any retention exception on request.

7. Accountability and contact

Rapolus must provide reasonable information needed to demonstrate these processing obligations, subject to confidentiality and other customers' security. Any further audit arrangements, scope and reasonable costs must be agreed; unrestricted access to shared systems is not granted. These terms do not limit statutory inspection powers or mandatory rights.
