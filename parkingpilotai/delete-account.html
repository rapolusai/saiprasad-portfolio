Delete your ParkingPilotAI account

This page explains how anyone who uses the ParkingPilotAI app (owners, managers and parking agents) can ask for their account and its personal data to be deleted.

1. How to ask

  • In the app, open the menu and choose Delete my account, which brings you here.
  • Email supportrapolu@gmail.com with the subject “ParkingPilotAI account deletion”. Include your username and the name of your parking place.
  • Managers and parking agents can also ask their owner, who can remove their access at once from Settings › Team.

We reply to confirm we have the request. We may ask you to confirm it from the mobile number or email on the account, so that nobody else can delete it.

2. What we delete

  • Your login: username, password, PIN and registered devices.
  • Your name, mobile number and recovery contact.
  • For an owner: the place’s account, team invitations, settings and payment QR.

We complete the deletion within 30 days of the confirmed request.

3. What we keep, and for how long

Some records must be kept by law or to settle disputes: parking entries and exits, payments, invoices, subscription purchases and audit history. We keep them only as long as tax and other applicable laws require (generally up to 8 years for financial records), with your name and mobile number removed where the law allows, and then delete them.

4. Customers who received a receipt

Vehicle owners do not have ParkingPilotAI accounts. To have a mobile number removed from a parking place’s receipts, contact that parking place or email us with the number and the place’s name.
