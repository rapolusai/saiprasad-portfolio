Cancellation and Refund Policy

This policy applies to ParkingPilotAI plans (Starter, Standard and Pro) and to WhatsApp and Print credit packs. All of them are bought inside the app through Google Play.

1. Free trial

A new parking place gets a 5-day free trial when it registers (one per owner mobile number). No payment details are needed for the trial and nothing is charged when it ends. To keep using ParkingPilotAI after the trial, the owner chooses and buys a plan.

2. Plans renew automatically

Starter is monthly only. Standard and Pro offer monthly, 3-month or 12-month subscriptions where available at Google Play checkout. The price and billing period are shown before you pay. A plan renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. Any future price changes follow Google Play's applicable notice and consent process.

3. How to cancel

Cancel in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › ParkingPilotAI › Cancel subscription. You can cancel at any time. The plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and it is not renewed after that. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

4. Refunds

Payments are taken by Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play’s refund policy. Within 48 hours of a purchase you can ask Google for a refund directly from your Google Play order history. After 48 hours, email us and we will review the request. We normally refund:

  • a duplicate or accidental purchase of the same plan;
  • a WhatsApp or Print credit pack bought by mistake, if none of its credits were used and the request is made within 7 days;
  • a period in which ParkingPilotAI was unavailable because of a fault on our side.

A plan period that has already been used, and used or partly used credits, are not normally refunded, except where the law requires it. Approved refunds are made by Google Play to the original payment method; your bank may take a few working days to show it.

5. Payment problems

A plan or credit pack is activated only after Google Play confirms the payment. If money was taken but the plan or credits did not appear, contact us with the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and we will fix it or refund it.

6. How to contact us

Email support@rapolu.in with the parking place name, the Google Play order number, the payment date, the amount and the reason. You may also call +91 91774 15553. Never send a password, PIN, OTP, CVV, or complete bank or card details.

7. Review, outages and account closure

Cancellation stops a future renewal; it is not itself a refund request. Closing a ParkingPilotAI account does not cancel its Google Play subscription. For a provider-caused outage refund, include the affected place and outage period. We investigate the service impact and explain the period and amount covered by any approved refund. This policy does not promise a fixed uptime percentage or automatic service credits.

We may request relevant purchase or usage records to review eligibility, but never payment credentials. Refund eligibility and processing depend on Google Play rules and applicable law. Google's decision and payment-processing times may differ by purchase and payment method. A refund is not guaranteed solely because a request is submitted within 48 hours. Nothing in this policy restricts a mandatory refund or other remedy available under law.

For Google Play's current rules and request process, see Google Play refund guidance.
