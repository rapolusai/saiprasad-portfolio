Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ParkingPilotAI, operated by Rapolus in Hyderabad, Telangana, India (“ParkingPilotAI”, “we”, “us”), handles information when customers and authorised team members use the product.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the feature used, we may collect account and business details, authorised user names and mobile numbers, role and location assignments, vehicle category and registration number, optional receipt-recipient mobile number, parking entry and exit timestamps, pricing and collection records, subscription and acknowledgement-credit usage, device and security logs, crash reports, support communications, and payment status or transaction references.

Vehicle numbers. When a vehicle number is recorded, it is saved with the parking entry to identify the vehicle, check for duplicate active entries and match its exit.

Receipt-recipient mobile numbers. A customer mobile number is optional and is collected and saved with the entry only when a WhatsApp receipt is requested, so that the receipt can be sent. If WhatsApp is not selected, no receipt-recipient mobile number is collected or saved. This does not refer to the separate mobile numbers used for staff or business accounts.

Camera. Agents can scan a number plate with the device camera. The plate is read on the device; the camera picture is not uploaded or stored by ParkingPilotAI. Only the vehicle number that the agent confirms is saved. The camera is also used to scan receipt QR codes at exit.

Bluetooth. Bluetooth is used only to connect to a receipt printer chosen by the user. It is not used to find the device’s location.

Notifications. The app can show notifications about the account, plan and operations. They can be turned off in the device settings.

We do not ask for or store a customer’s UPI PIN, complete card number, CVV, online-banking password, or OTP. Plans and credit packs are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment details.

2. How we use information

  • Provide vehicle entry, exit, receipt, pricing, collection, team, and reporting functions.
  • Deliver an acknowledgement through the channel selected by the parking operator.
  • Confirm payments, grant purchased credits, reconcile usage, and handle refunds or disputes.
  • Authenticate users, enforce roles, protect tenants and locations, detect misuse, and maintain audit history.
  • Support customers, improve reliability, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Processing purposes and customer responsibility

Parking owners determine why vehicle, staff and receipt-recipient data is entered for their parking operations. Rapolus processes this operational data on the customer's documented instructions under the Data Processing Agreement. Rapolus also determines the purposes of its own account administration, billing, fraud prevention and legal records. Each party must establish a lawful basis under the law applicable to its processing; a service contract or operational interest does not by itself replace consent or another basis required by Indian law.

Parking owners must give appropriate notices, obtain consent where required, and enter only necessary data. Rights and duties under India's data-protection framework apply according to their notified commencement and applicability; this policy does not claim certification or complete legal compliance.

4. Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by vetted service providers only as needed to deliver the service:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS), Mumbai region, India: servers, database and receipt images.
  • Google Play: purchases of plans and credit packs.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): WhatsApp receipts and messages, when the parking operator chooses WhatsApp.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports (device model, Android version, app version and the error), used to fix problems.
  • Anthropic: answers to questions asked in the in-app AskAI assistant. Questions are sent with summary figures for the parking place (counts and totals); vehicle numbers and customer mobile numbers are not sent.
  • Email and support providers, used to answer support requests and to store the weekly archive.

We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users and the service, or during a lawful business restructuring. We do not sell personal information or mobile opt-in data.

5. WhatsApp, SMS, and opt-out

When the parking operator chooses WhatsApp (or SMS, when available) for a receipt, the customer’s mobile number is used to send that receipt. Messaging may also be used for transactional acknowledgements, receipts, account alerts, subscription and billing updates, and support messages when the parking operator has a lawful basis to provide the recipient number. Messages may appear from Rapolus or another approved sender/profile configured with the messaging provider.

Marketing messages are not sent unless separately opted in. Recipients may opt out of non-essential messages by replying STOP where supported or by contacting support@rapolu.in. Opting out may not stop essential service, security, legal, billing, or transaction messages.

6. Storage, retention, and security

We apply role-based access, tenant and location isolation, encryption in transit, restricted administration, audit logging, and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. No internet service is risk-free. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored in India.

  • Customer mobile numbers on receipts: optional. When entered, the full number is kept for 90 days (to send the receipt and settle any dispute), then reduced to the last 4 digits in the app and its database. The weekly Excel archive, kept privately by ParkingPilotAI for records and disputes, keeps the full number for as long as financial records are kept. A short record of each WhatsApp receipt sent (the number, time and delivery status) is kept as proof of delivery for as long as financial records are kept. Removed earlier on request (see Delete your account, section 4).
  • Parking entries, exits and payments: processed in a weekly archive run to manage active database storage. For theatre locations, entries older than 7 days are eligible for archiving. Where exit is mandatory, only entries for vehicles whose exit has been successfully recorded are archived; entries still awaiting mandatory exit are not removed by this routine. Eligible entries are archived and removed from the active database, rather than permanently erased from all storage. Because this runs weekly, an eligible entry may remain in the database until the next archive run. The archive is held securely by ParkingPilotAI for records and disputes, subject to the retention limits and review described below. Historical entries removed from the active database may no longer appear in the app; contact support for archive-related requests.
  • Receipt links: a digital receipt opens from a private link. Anyone who has the link can view that one receipt, which shows the place, the time, the vehicle type and only the last 4 characters of the vehicle number.
  • User accounts: kept while the account is active, and deleted within 30 days of a confirmed deletion request (see below).

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how your personal data is used. Operational and financial records may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to resolve transactions and disputes. Receipt-recipient mobile numbers are optional and collected only for requested WhatsApp receipts; choosing another receipt channel does not require a customer mobile number.

To delete your account, see Delete your account, or choose Delete my account in the app menu.

8. Children

ParkingPilotAI is a business operations product and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly create end-user accounts for children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, or provider changes. The effective date on this page will be revised when material changes are published.

10. Privacy requests and grievances

Send privacy questions, correction or deletion requests, consent-withdrawal requests, and grievances to the Rapolus privacy support contact at support@rapolu.in, or call +91 91774 15553. Include the parking place, your relationship to it and the request. We may verify identity or authority before disclosing or changing records. Please do not send passwords, PINs, OTPs, or complete banking credentials.

We will explain the outcome or any lawful retention exception and handle requests within the periods required by applicable law. You may ask for an unresolved issue to be escalated within Rapolus and use any regulator or dispute-resolution route available under applicable law. This support contact is not a claim that a statutory Data Protection Officer has been appointed.

Processing locations, archives and incidents

The primary application hosting described above is in AWS Mumbai. Messaging, payment, crash-reporting, AI and support providers may process their relevant data in other countries under their own service arrangements. Primary hosting in India does not mean every provider processes all data only in India. Transfers must comply with restrictions applicable at the time.

Masking a mobile number in the active database does not remove historical archives or delivery logs. As described in section 6, those copies can retain the full number for records and disputes. A financial-record retention period does not automatically require every personal identifier to be retained. Requests for earlier removal or masking will be assessed across active records, archives and provider records, subject to an identified legal or dispute-related retention need. We will explain applicable exceptions. Archived records are not intended for marketing or routine operational reuse.

Backup copies may remain until their ordinary replacement or expiry, with restricted access; any restoration must respect completed deletion requests. Suspected unauthorised disclosure should be reported promptly to support. Rapolus will investigate, take containment measures and notify affected customers, individuals or authorities where applicable law requires, using the information available at the time.
