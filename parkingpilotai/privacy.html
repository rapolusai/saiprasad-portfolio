Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ParkingPilotAI, operated by Rapolu`s in Hyderabad, Telangana, India (“ParkingPilotAI”, “we”, “us”), handles information when customers and authorised team members use the product.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the feature used, we may collect account and business details, authorised user names and mobile numbers, role and location assignments, vehicle category and registration number, optional receipt-recipient mobile number, parking entry and exit timestamps, pricing and collection records, subscription and acknowledgement-credit usage, device and security logs, crash reports, support communications, and payment status or transaction references.

Camera. Agents can scan a number plate with the device camera. The plate is read on the device; the camera picture is not uploaded or stored by ParkingPilotAI. Only the vehicle number that the agent confirms is saved. The camera is also used to scan receipt QR codes at exit.

Bluetooth. Bluetooth is used only to connect to a receipt printer chosen by the user. It is not used to find the device’s location.

Notifications. The app can show notifications about the account, plan and operations. They can be turned off in the device settings.

We do not ask for or store a customer’s UPI PIN, complete card number, CVV, online-banking password, or OTP. Plans and credit packs are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment details.

2. How we use information

  • Provide vehicle entry, exit, receipt, pricing, collection, team, and reporting functions.
  • Deliver an acknowledgement through the channel selected by the parking operator.
  • Confirm payments, grant purchased credits, reconcile usage, and handle refunds or disputes.
  • Authenticate users, enforce roles, protect tenants and locations, detect misuse, and maintain audit history.
  • Support customers, improve reliability, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Legal basis and customer responsibility

We process information to provide the contracted service, protect legitimate operational and security interests, comply with law, and act on consent where required. Parking owners control the operational data entered by their authorised users and must provide appropriate notices and obtain any legally required consent from vehicle owners or receipt recipients.

4. Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by vetted service providers only as needed to deliver the service:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS), Mumbai region, India: servers, database and receipt images.
  • Google Play: purchases of plans and credit packs.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): WhatsApp receipts and messages, when the parking operator chooses WhatsApp.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports (device model, Android version, app version and the error), used to fix problems.
  • Anthropic: answers to questions asked in the in-app AskAI assistant. Questions are sent with summary figures for the parking place (counts and totals); vehicle numbers and customer mobile numbers are not sent.
  • Email and support providers, used to answer support requests and to store the weekly archive.

We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users and the service, or during a lawful business restructuring. We do not sell personal information or mobile opt-in data.

5. WhatsApp, SMS, and opt-out

When the parking operator chooses WhatsApp (or SMS, when available) for a receipt, the customer’s mobile number is used to send that receipt. Messaging may also be used for transactional acknowledgements, receipts, account alerts, subscription and billing updates, and support messages when the parking operator has a lawful basis to provide the recipient number. Messages may appear from Rapolus or another approved sender/profile configured with the messaging provider.

Marketing messages are not sent unless separately opted in. Recipients may opt out of non-essential messages by replying STOP where supported or by contacting support@rapolu.in. Opting out may not stop essential service, security, legal, billing, or transaction messages.

6. Storage, retention, and security

We apply role-based access, tenant and location isolation, encryption in transit, restricted administration, audit logging, and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. No internet service is risk-free. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored in India.

  • Customer mobile numbers on receipts: optional. When entered, the full number is kept for 90 days (to send the receipt and settle any dispute), then reduced to the last 4 digits in the app and its database. The weekly Excel archive, kept privately by ParkingPilotAI for records and disputes, keeps the full number for as long as financial records are kept. A short record of each WhatsApp receipt sent (the number, time and delivery status) is kept as proof of delivery for as long as financial records are kept. Removed earlier on request (see Delete your account, section 4).
  • Parking entries, exits and payments: kept in the app for 7 days after the vehicle exits and the collection is settled. They are then moved into a weekly Excel archive held securely by ParkingPilotAI, and kept as long as tax and other laws require (generally up to 8 years for financial records).
  • Receipt links: a digital receipt opens from a private link. Anyone who has the link can view that one receipt, which shows the place, the time, the vehicle type and only the last 4 characters of the vehicle number.
  • User accounts: kept while the account is active, and deleted within 30 days of a confirmed deletion request (see below).

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how your personal data is used. Operational and financial records may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to resolve transactions and disputes. Receipt-recipient mobile numbers are optional unless the parking operator has a lawful operational requirement.

To delete your account, see Delete your account, or choose Delete my account in the app menu.

8. Children

ParkingPilotAI is a business operations product and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly create end-user accounts for children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, or provider changes. The effective date on this page will be revised when material changes are published.
