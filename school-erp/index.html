How it works

From admission to the parent's phone.

  1. 1
    Set up the school

    An administrator configures classes, sections and staff access.

  2. 2
    Add students

    Admissions and existing students are recorded with their details.

  3. 3
    Run the day

    Staff record attendance and fees as part of the daily routine.

  4. 4
    Review and share

    Administrators review records and keep parents updated.
