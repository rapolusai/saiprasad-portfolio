How it works

From registration to the daily check-in.

  1. 1
    Set up the club

    An administrator configures the centre, trainers and staff access.

  2. 2
    Register members

    Front-desk staff add members and issue QR ID cards.

  3. 3
    Check in daily

    Members are scanned in and attendance history builds automatically.

  4. 4
    Review the business

    Fees, expenses and activity are reviewed from the dashboard and reports.
