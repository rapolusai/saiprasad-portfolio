Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the ApartmentCare Android app and its server keep, why, who can see it and how to have it removed. ApartmentCare is operated by Rapolus IT Services, Hyderabad, Telangana, India (“we”, “us”). It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Google Play's User Data policy.

1. What ApartmentCare is

ApartmentCare helps a residential apartment association manage maintenance dues, payments, expenses, notices, polls, complaints, bookings and residents. Each apartment's records belong to that apartment's association. People sign in as an Admin, a Treasurer or a Resident (owner or tenant) of one apartment and see only what their role allows for that apartment.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, optional email address, your flat, and whether you are the owner or a tenant. Your PIN is stored only as a one-way hash; we cannot read it.
  • Apartment details entered by the Admin: apartment name, address and PIN code, blocks and flats, committee titles, vendors and contacts, and the payment account residents pay maintenance into.
  • Financial records of the apartment: maintenance bills, payment declarations, the payment proofs you choose to upload, receipts, reversals, expenses with their bills, and reports.
  • Community records: notices and their read or acknowledgement status, poll votes, bookings, complaints and their history, documents, help requests, and vehicle registration numbers entered for your flat.
  • Subscription records for an apartment: plan, invoices, payments recorded by us and referral codes.
  • Technical and security records: sign-in attempts, the time of each request, the last sign-in time, and an identifier of the network address a request came from. These protect accounts against PIN guessing and help us investigate problems.

Please do not upload information about another person unless you are authorised to, and never put passwords, PINs, OTPs, card numbers or banking credentials in a proof, comment or support request.

3. What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, call logs, SMS, microphone or camera access. The app's only Android permission is internet access.
  • No photos or files other than the ones you choose through the Android file picker to attach.
  • No advertising identifier, no advertisements, and no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries.
  • No card numbers, UPI PINs or banking passwords. ApartmentCare does not process payments between residents and their association.

4. How we use information

  • To sign you in and give you the functions of your role in your apartment.
  • To keep the apartment's accounts: bills, payments, verification, receipts, expenses and reports.
  • To run the apartment's day-to-day operations: notices, polls, bookings, complaints, documents and help requests.
  • To manage the apartment's ApartmentCare subscription, free month and referrals.
  • To protect accounts and data, keep an audit trail, fix faults and answer support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Who can see your information

  • You see your own records and the information your apartment shares.
  • Your apartment's Admin and Treasurer see residents' details, payments, proofs and requests for their own apartment only, because they run the association's accounts and verify payments.
  • Other residents see shared notices, polls, public reports with apartment totals, expenses the Admin makes visible and the directory the Admin chooses to share. They never see your payment proofs or private requests.
  • The ApartmentCare team (a small number of our staff) can see, for every apartment, its name and city, the Admin's name and mobile number, the number of flats and members, the subscription status and payments, and when someone from the apartment last signed in, so that we can support apartments and manage subscriptions. The team cannot see residents' payment proofs, requests or other records through the app.
  • We, as the service operator, can access the server to keep it running, fix faults and act on verified deletion or legal requests.

We do not share personal information with any other company, except a hosting provider that stores the data for us under contract, or where the law requires it.

6. Storage and security

Information is stored on a server in India and sent only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. PINs are hashed, repeated wrong PINs lock sign-in for a while, and every request is checked against the signed-in person's apartment and role. Attachments can be opened only by people allowed to see the record they belong to. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account details are kept while your account is active.
  • Financial records (bills, payments, receipts, expenses and the audit trail behind them) are kept for as long as the association is required to keep its accounts, because they are the association's records, not only yours.
  • Technical and security records are kept for a short period and then removed automatically.
  • When an apartment's subscription is unpaid, its access pauses but its records are not deleted. An apartment and all its records are deleted when its Admin asks us to.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: My profile → Delete my account (also under More → Help), then confirm with your PIN. The account is deleted straight away.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on the account deletion page. We verify the request and complete it within 30 days.

When an account is deleted it is closed immediately and signed out on every device. Your name, mobile number, email and PIN are removed and your flat is released. Financial records you were part of stay with the apartment, shown as “Former resident” with the flat number instead of your name. An apartment's only Admin must make another member Admin before deleting their own account, so the apartment is not left without one. To remove a whole apartment and all its records, its Admin should contact us.

9. Your rights

You can view and correct your name and email in the app. You can also ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct or complete it, to delete it, or to nominate someone to act for you, by writing to support@rapolu.in. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Children

ApartmentCare is intended for adult residents and apartment office-bearers. It is not directed at children under 18, and children should not create accounts. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was submitted should contact us so we can remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date, and significant changes are announced in the app.
