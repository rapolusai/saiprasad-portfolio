Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ApartmentCare, an apartment community application operated by Rapolu`s in Hyderabad, Telangana, India (“ApartmentCare”, “we”, “us”), handles information when people use the app or contact us. The app is in development; actual data practices may depend on the build and enabled features.

1. Information that may be processed

Depending on the workflow and build, ApartmentCare may process account and profile details such as name and phone number; apartment/community name and address; flat, resident, and role information; resident invitation and access records; maintenance issues, comments, status updates, and user-selected attachments; notices and bookings; vehicle details submitted for community services; maintenance-payment declarations and related transaction records; referral or subscription-related records when those features are enabled; and technical, security, and audit events.

Only provide information needed for the relevant apartment workflow. Do not submit passwords, PINs, OTPs, card security codes, or complete payment credentials in a support request or attachment.

2. Permissions and attachments

The Android app may let you choose a file or photo through the device’s system picker for an applicable feature. Information you select and submit can be stored and shown to authorised users for that apartment workflow. Do not attach information about another person unless you are authorised to do so.

3. How information is used

  • Provide account access and resident or administrator functions.
  • Organise apartment, flat, and resident records.
  • Submit, assign, communicate about, and follow maintenance issues or community services.
  • Maintain necessary financial, referral, and subscription records for the relevant features.
  • Protect accounts and apartment data, maintain audit history, troubleshoot issues, and respond to support requests.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements and resolve disputes.

4. Roles, apartments, and sharing

ApartmentCare is designed to show information to users authorised for the relevant apartment and role. Apartment administrators and residents should use the app only for their assigned community. We do not treat information as public merely because it is entered into a community workflow. Users should not enter another apartment’s information or try to access records they are not authorised to view.

5. Providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers needed to operate a particular deployed build. The development/test build is not evidence that any production provider, online payment, OTP, push-notification, or messaging integration is configured. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or in connection with a lawful business transfer.

6. Retention and security

We use access controls and other safeguards appropriate to the deployed service, but no internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. Records may be retained for account operation, community history, security, financial recordkeeping, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. The precise retention period depends on the record and applicable requirements. We will update this policy when production providers, retention periods, or material data practices are finalized.

7. Account and data deletion requests

You may request deletion of your ApartmentCare account and associated personal data by emailing rajinirapolus@gamil.com from your registered address or phone-contact email, if available. Include “ApartmentCare deletion request” and your account phone number; never include your password, PIN, or OTP. We may ask for reasonable information to verify the request. Some records may be retained where required by law, for security, or to resolve financial transactions or disputes; we will explain applicable retention when responding. This public request channel does not itself add an in-app delete-account feature—availability of that feature must be confirmed in the app before release.

8. Children

ApartmentCare is intended for apartment residents and administrators, not children under the minimum age required by applicable law to create an account. A parent or guardian who believes a child’s information was submitted should contact us.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product and its providers are finalized. The effective date above will change when a revised version is published. For privacy questions or requests, email rajinirapolus@gamil.com or call +91 99636 59143.
