Delete your ApartmentCare account

Anyone who uses ApartmentCare (Admins, Treasurers, owners and tenants) can delete their account and personal data, with or without the app.

1. In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ApartmentCare and sign in.
  2. Go to My profile (or More → Help) and tap Delete my account.
  3. Read what is removed and what is kept, enter your PIN and confirm.

The account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every device.

2. Without the app

Email support@rapolu.in with the subject “ApartmentCare account deletion request” and include:

  • the mobile number you sign in with,
  • your apartment's name, and
  • your flat number, if you have one.

We confirm the request comes from the account holder, usually by calling that mobile number back or checking with your apartment's Admin, and then delete the account within 30 days. We reply to tell you when it is done. We will never ask for your PIN or OTP.

3. What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email address and PIN.
  • Your sign-in on every device.
  • Your link to your flat, so the flat can be given to the next resident.

4. What is kept, and why

Maintenance bills, payments, receipts, expenses and their audit trail that you were part of are the apartment association's accounts, so they stay with the apartment. In those records your name is replaced by “Former resident” and your flat number. They are kept for as long as the association must keep its accounts. Short-lived security logs are removed automatically.

5. Admins and whole apartments

If you are your apartment's only Admin, first make another member Admin (Apartment → Team roles), then delete your account. To delete an entire apartment and all of its records, the Admin should email support@rapolu.in with the apartment name.
