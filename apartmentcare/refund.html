Cancellation and Refund Policy

This page explains how an apartment can stop its ApartmentCare subscription and when we refund subscription money. It covers only the subscription an apartment association pays to Rapolus IT Services. Maintenance and other dues residents pay to their own association are not handled by ApartmentCare; questions about those go to the association.

1. Free first month

Every new apartment gets its first month free. Nothing is charged during the free month, and no payment details are needed to start. If the apartment does not continue, nothing is owed.

2. Cancelling

The subscription is monthly. The apartment's Admin can cancel at any time by emailing support@rapolu.in from the Admin's registered contact, or calling us, with the apartment name. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month already paid for; the apartment keeps full access until then and no further invoices are raised. If an invoice is simply left unpaid, access pauses until it is paid, and the apartment's records are kept.

3. Refunds

  • Charged twice or charged by mistake: fully refunded once we confirm it.
  • Paid for a month after cancelling in time: fully refunded.
  • Service unavailable for a long period through our fault: a refund or credit in proportion to the time lost, on request.
  • First invoice waived by a referral after it was already paid: we review it and refund or credit that invoice.
  • Part-used months: a month already started is not refunded when an apartment cancels, except in the cases above.

Approved refunds are sent back to the account the payment came from, normally within 7 working days of approval. Your bank or payment app may take a few more days to show it.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email support@rapolu.in within 30 days of the payment with the apartment name, the date, the amount and the transaction reference. Never send a card number, CVV, UPI PIN, banking password, PIN or OTP. We reply within 3 working days.

5. Your rights

This policy does not limit any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other applicable law.
