Cancellation and Refund Policy

This page explains how an apartment's Admin can cancel the ApartmentCare subscription and how refunds work. The subscription is bought by the Admin in the app through Google Play. Residents, Treasurers and other members are never charged by ApartmentCare. Maintenance and other dues residents pay to their own association are not handled by ApartmentCare; questions about those go to the association.

1. 5-day free trial

Every new apartment starts with a 5-day free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. If the Admin does not subscribe, nothing is owed; access pauses after the trial and the apartment's records are kept.

2. Cancelling

The subscription renews every month until it is cancelled. The Admin cancels it at any time in the Google Play Store app: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ApartmentCare → Cancel subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the apartment keeps full access until the end of the month already paid for. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription.

3. Refunds

Payments are processed by Google Play, so refunds are made through Google Play to the payment method that was used. The Admin can ask Google Play for a refund directly; Google Play's own refund policy and time limits apply. You can also contact us, and we will refund through Google Play in these cases:

  • Charged twice or charged by mistake: fully refunded once we confirm it.
  • Service unavailable for a long period through our fault: a refund in proportion to the time lost, on request.
  • Part-used months: a month already started is not refunded when the Admin cancels, except in the cases above.

We issue approved refunds within 7 working days of approval. Google Play and your bank or payment app may take a few more days to show them.

4. How to ask us for a refund

Email support@rapolu.in within 30 days of the payment with the apartment name, the date, the amount and the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your Google Play purchase email). Never send a card number, CVV, UPI PIN, banking password, PIN or OTP. We reply within 3 working days.

5. Your rights

This policy does not limit any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 or other applicable law.
