Before you write

Forgot your PIN or locked out? Use Forgot PIN? on the sign-in screen, or ask your apartment's Admin. Payment not approved yet? Your apartment's Treasurer or Admin verifies payments; ApartmentCare cannot approve them for you. Subscription or invoice question? Write to us with the apartment name.

Account and data deletion

Delete your account in the app under My profile → Delete my account, or follow the account deletion page if you no longer have the app. Some financial records stay with your apartment, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Keep credentials private

Never email a password, PIN, OTP, CVV, UPI PIN, full card number, or online-banking password.
