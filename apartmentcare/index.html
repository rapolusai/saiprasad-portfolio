Features

The everyday work of an apartment association.

Everything below is built into the Android app. Each person sees only what their role allows, and only for their own apartment.

01

Apartment setup

Blocks, flats, owners and tenants, committee titles, vendors and useful contacts. Invite links can be paused, rotated or revoked by the Admin.

02

Resident onboarding

Residents join with the invite, pick their flat and say whether they are the owner or a tenant. The Admin reviews each request and can reject it with a reason.

03

Maintenance billing

Monthly bills by flat, late fees, opening dues, special contributions and other income, with a preview before anything is billed and reminders for outstanding dues.

04

Payments and receipts

Residents pay the association directly (cash, UPI or bank) and record the payment with a proof. The Treasurer or Admin verifies it before a receipt is issued. Mistakes are reversed with an audit trail, never silently deleted.

05

Expenses and reports

Expenses with bills attached, recurring expenses, a cashbook and monthly reports that can be shared as PDF. The Admin decides which expenses residents can see.

06

Notices and polls

Notices for everyone, a block or selected flats, scheduled in advance, with read and acknowledgement tracking, plus polls and an in-app inbox.

07

Complaints and services

Common issues and private complaints with status, history, vendor and expected date, and preventive service schedules for lifts, tanks and other equipment.

08

Bookings

Residents request the clubhouse or other common areas, and the Admin approves after checking availability. Bookings are free; no deposit is collected in the app.

09

Vehicles, documents and help

A vehicle directory for parking, public and private apartment documents, and private help requests to the apartment's team.

Roles

One app, a login for each role.

  1. A
    Admin

    Sets up the apartment, approves residents, bills maintenance, publishes notices, manages bookings, complaints and the team, and holds the apartment's subscription.

  2. T
    Treasurer

    Verifies payment proofs, issues receipts, records expenses and keeps the cashbook and reports.

  3. R
    Residents

    Owners and tenants see their dues, record payments, download receipts, raise complaints, book common areas and read notices.

  4. ✓
    Checks and balances

    The person who records a payment cannot approve it, and approved money is corrected only by an audited reversal.

The plan is based on the number of flats configured for the apartment. The Admin sees the plan, the free-month end date and each month's amount under More → Subscription & billing. If the subscription is not paid after the free month, the apartment's access pauses until it is paid; its records are not deleted. Referral rewards, when offered, are shown in the app and apply only after our team verifies each referred apartment.

Maintenance money stays with your association

Residents pay maintenance directly to their association's account. ApartmentCare does not collect, hold or transfer maintenance money and does not check payments with any bank. A payment counts only after the apartment's Treasurer or Admin verifies it. The app never asks for card numbers, UPI PINs or banking passwords.

Privacy and your data

Only what the apartment needs.

ApartmentCare keeps your name, mobile number, flat and the records you create for your apartment. It has no advertisements, no analytics and no tracking, and it asks for no location, contacts or camera permission. Your PIN is stored only as a one-way hash, and every connection is encrypted.

Delete your account at any time

In the app, open My profile → Delete my account and confirm with your PIN. Without the app, use the account deletion page. Read the Privacy Policy for what is removed and what the association keeps.
