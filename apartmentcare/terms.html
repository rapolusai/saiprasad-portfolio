1. The service

ApartmentCare is a tool for residential apartment associations to manage apartment setup, residents, maintenance billing, payment declarations and verification, receipts, expenses, reports, notices, polls, complaints, service schedules, bookings, vehicles, documents and help requests. We may improve, change or retire features; we will give reasonable notice of changes that materially reduce what a paying apartment receives.

2. Accounts and roles

  • You must be 18 or older and a resident, owner or authorised office-bearer of the apartment you join.
  • Sign in with your own mobile number and a private 6-digit PIN. Do not share your account or PIN; you are responsible for activity under it.
  • The person who registers an apartment becomes its Admin and confirms they are authorised by the association to do so. The Admin approves residents, assigns the Treasurer and other roles, and is responsible for removing people who should no longer have access.
  • Use only the apartment and role you were given. Do not try to see another apartment's records, impersonate anyone or get around the app's permissions.

4. Maintenance money and payments between residents and the association

Residents pay maintenance and other dues directly to their association. ApartmentCare does not collect, hold or transfer that money and does not verify payments with any bank or payment provider. A payment declared in the app is only a claim until the apartment's Treasurer or Admin verifies it; the person who declared a payment cannot approve it. Approved payments and expenses are corrected only by audited reversals. The association, not ApartmentCare, is responsible for its accounts, collections, receipts and any taxes or statutory filings.

5. Subscription, free trial and access

  • Each apartment has one monthly subscription, based on the number of flats configured: up to 10 flats ₹99, 11 to 30 flats ₹149, 31 to 50 flats ₹249 per month. Only the apartment's Admin buys it; Residents, Treasurers and other members are never charged by ApartmentCare.
  • Each new apartment starts with a 5-day free trial. The Admin can see the plan and the trial end date under More → Subscription & billing.
  • The Admin buys the subscription in the app through Google Play, using the payment methods Google Play offers. Google Play's terms apply to the purchase. The subscription renews automatically every month until the Admin cancels it in Google Play; cancelling stops the next renewal and access continues until the end of the month already paid for.
  • The subscription belongs to the apartment, not to one person. If the Admin role is handed to someone else, an active subscription carries on and the apartment keeps full access; the new Admin does not need to pay again while it is active. Google Play keeps billing the Google account that bought it until that person cancels it in Google Play. After it ends, the new Admin can subscribe from their own account. A former Admin who is still paying should cancel in Google Play only once the new Admin is ready to subscribe, so that access does not pause.
  • If there is no active subscription after the trial, access for the whole apartment pauses until the Admin subscribes. The Admin can still open the subscription screen and sign out. Records are not deleted because of non-payment.
  • Referral rewards may be offered from time to time; any reward and its conditions are shown in the app.
  • We may change prices with at least 30 days' notice in the app and as Google Play requires. A new price applies from the next renewal after the notice period.
