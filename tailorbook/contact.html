Before you write

Moving to a new phone? Turn on Android backup to your Google account on the old phone, then restore it on the new one. Lost your records? Your data is kept only on your phone, so we cannot see or recover it. WhatsApp not opening? Check that WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed and that the customer's phone number is correct.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete. Delete a customer, order or expense inside the app, or delete everything by uninstalling Tailor Book or clearing its storage in your phone's settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Keep your information private

Never email a password, OTP, card number or banking details. We will never ask for them.
