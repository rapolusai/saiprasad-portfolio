Tailor Book is a simple Android app for tailor shops, ladies' tailors and boutiques. Save each customer's measurements once, track every order from cutting to delivery, record advances and balances, and send “your clothes are ready” on WhatsApp in one tap. It works offline, needs no sign-up and is available in English and Telugu.

Features

The everyday work of a tailor shop.

Everything below is built into the Android app.

01

Today at a glance

Orders due today, overdue orders, orders ready for pickup, today's collection and total pending balance as soon as you open the app.

02

Measurements

Each customer's measurements for every item, in inches, with special instructions. Next time the order form fills them in for you.

03

Orders

Received → Cutting → Stitching → Ready → Delivered, with delivery dates, notes and search by name, phone or order number.

04

Payments

Advance and balance payments for every order, and how much each customer still owes.

05

WhatsApp in one tap

Send “your clothes are ready” messages and order receipts through WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business.

06

Monthly accounts

Expenses like material, worker wages, rent and electricity, with money collected and profit for each month.

Privacy and your data

Your shop's records stay with you.

Tailor Book keeps everything on your phone. It has no account and no server, so your customers' details never reach us. There are no advertisements, no analytics and no tracking. Turn on Android backup to keep a copy in your own Google account.

Delete your data at any time

Delete customers, orders and expenses in the app, or uninstall Tailor Book to remove everything. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
