1. The app

Tailor Book is a record-keeping tool for tailor shops and boutiques: customers and their measurements, orders and delivery dates, payments, expenses and monthly totals, with shortcuts to call or WhatsApp a customer. It works offline and stores everything on your phone. We may improve, change or retire features in future versions.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro continues until the end of the month already paid for.

  • Price changes: we may change the price with notice in the app and as Google Play requires; a new price applies from a renewal after the notice period.
  • Pro is tied to the Google account that bought it. If Pro ends, the app returns to the free plan; your records are never deleted because of non-payment.
