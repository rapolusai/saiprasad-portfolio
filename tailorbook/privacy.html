Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Tailor Book Android app stores, where it is kept, who can see it and how to delete it. Tailor Book is published by Rapolus IT Services, Hyderabad, Telangana, India (“we”, “us”). It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Google Play's User Data policy.

1. What Tailor Book is

Tailor Book is an order book for tailor shops, ladies' tailors and boutiques. The shop owner uses it to save customers' measurements, track orders and delivery dates, record advance and balance payments, note shop expenses and send customers WhatsApp messages. There is no sign-up and no account: the app works fully offline on the shop owner's phone.

2. The short version

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. Tailor Book has no server and never sends your shop's records to us or to anyone else. Its only network use is Google Play's billing library, which talks to Google Play about the optional Pro subscription.
  • We, Rapolus IT Services, never receive your shop's data or your customers' data, and we cannot see, recover or delete it for you.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • If you subscribe to Pro, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card, UPI or bank details.

3. Information stored in the app

Only the information you type into the app is stored, in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Shop details: the shop name and phone number you enter in Settings, used in the messages you send to customers.
  • Customers: name, phone number and any notes you add.
  • Measurements: each customer's measurements by item (shirt, pant, blouse, chudidar, kurta, lehenga and others) and any special instructions.
  • Orders: item, quantity, the measurements used, price, order and delivery dates, status and notes.
  • Payments and expenses: advance and balance payments for each order, and shop expenses such as material, wages, rent and electricity.
  • Preferences: app language (English or Telugu) and colour theme.

Please enter only what your shop needs. Do not store passwords, card numbers, Aadhaar numbers or other sensitive information in notes.

4. What Tailor Book does not collect

  • The only Android permissions are the Google Play billing permission and network access, which Google Play's billing library uses to check and complete Pro subscription purchases. There is no location, contacts, call logs, SMS, camera, microphone or storage access.
  • No advertising identifier, no advertisements, and no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries.
  • No payment details. Tailor Book records amounts you received from customers; it does not process those payments.

5. Pro subscription and Google Play

Tailor Book is free for up to 25 new orders a month. The optional Tailor Book Pro monthly subscription is bought and paid for through Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms. To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play on your phone whether your Google account has an active subscription; Google Play answers with the subscription status and a purchase reference, which the app keeps on the phone. Google Play gives us order records for our accounts and refunds, such as the order number, date, product, price and country, but never your card, UPI or bank details. We do not link these records to the shop data in the app, which never leaves your phone.

6. Backup to your Google account

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include Tailor Book's data in your phone's backup to your own Google account, so it can be restored when you set up a new phone. This backup is created and stored by Google under your Google account and Google's Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn backup off or delete backups in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup.

7. Backup files you create

In Settings → Backup you can save a backup file of all your Tailor Book records (customers, measurements, orders, payments, expenses and shop details) and restore it later, for example on a new phone. You choose where the file is saved, such as Google Drive, your phone's Downloads folder or another app. The file is not encrypted and we never receive it, so keep it somewhere private and delete old copies you no longer need. Restoring a backup replaces the records on the phone with those in the file.

8. WhatsApp, phone and email

When you tap WhatsApp, Ready message or Send receipt, Tailor Book opens WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) with the customer's number and a ready-made message. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp, and what you send is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Tapping Call opens your phone's dialler, and tapping our support email opens your email app. Tailor Book itself does not send messages, make calls or upload anything.

9. Your customers' information

The customer details in Tailor Book belong to your shop. As the shop owner you decide what to record and you are responsible for collecting it fairly, using it only for your customers' orders, keeping your phone secure (for example with a screen lock) and deleting it when a customer asks. We do not process your customers' information because it never leaves your phone.

10. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • Delete one record: open the customer, order, payment or expense and use Delete. Deleting a customer also deletes their orders and payments.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Tailor Book → Storage → Clear storage. This cannot be undone.
  • Delete the Google backup: remove it from your Google account's backups as described in the Backup section.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot restore it if you delete the app or lose your phone without a backup.

11. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so use a screen lock. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your phone's software up to date.

12. Your rights

Because all information is on your device, you can view, correct and delete it directly in the app at any time. If you have a question about this policy or about your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, write to support@rapolu.in. We reply within 30 days.

13. Children

Tailor Book is a business tool for shop owners and is not directed at children under 18.

14. Future versions

If a future version adds features that collect or share information, such as advertising or online backup, we will update this policy and the Play Store data safety section before that version is released, and explain the change in the app.
