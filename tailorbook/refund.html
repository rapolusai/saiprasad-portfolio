Cancellation and Refund Policy

This page explains how to stop a Tailor Book Pro subscription and when subscription money is refunded. Tailor Book Pro is bought through Google Play, so Google Play takes the payment, handles renewals and sends refunds. Money your customers pay your shop is recorded in the app but never handled by Tailor Book.

1. Free plan and free trial

Tailor Book is free for up to 50 new orders a month, with no payment details needed. When a free trial of Pro is offered, nothing is charged during the trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged at all.

2. Cancelling

Pro renews every month until you cancel. Cancel at any time in Google Play: open the Play Store → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tailor Book Pro → Cancel subscription, or in the app go to Settings → Tailor Book Pro → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro keeps working until the end of the month already paid for, then the app returns to the free plan. Uninstalling the app does not cancel the subscription. Your records are never deleted because of cancellation or non-payment.

3. Refunds

  • Within 48 hours of a charge: you can ask Google for a refund directly at Google Play's refund request page; Google decides these requests.
  • Charged twice, or charged after cancelling in time: fully refunded once we confirm it.
  • Pro not working through our fault for a long period: a refund in proportion to the time lost, on request.
  • Part-used months: a month already started is not refunded when you cancel, except in the cases above.

Approved refunds are sent by Google Play to the payment method used for the purchase. Your bank or payment app may take a few days to show it.

4. How to ask us for a refund

Email support@rapolu.in within 30 days of the charge with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in the Google Play receipt email), the date and the amount. Never send a card number, CVV, UPI PIN, banking password or OTP. We reply within 3 working days.

5. Your rights

This policy does not limit any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, Google Play's policies or other applicable law.
